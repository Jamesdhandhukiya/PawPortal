Our Story

Founded in 2020, Paw Portal has grown from a small local initiative to a nationwide network of animal lovers and advocates. We believe every pet deserves a loving home and work tirelessly to make that vision a reality.

Our dedicated team of volunteers and staff work around the clock to rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome animals in need. We provide comprehensive medical care, behavioral training, and endless love to ensure each pet is ready for their forever home.

Professional veterinary care for all rescued animals
Thorough screening process for potential adopters
Post-adoption support and guidance
Community education and outreach programs
